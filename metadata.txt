about=<b>Raster Classification</b> guides you from the bands of a satellite image to a land cover map, in one panel of QGIS with four tabs.<br><br><b>Color composition</b>: load the bands of a Sentinel-2, Landsat or ASTER image (the satellite is recognised from the file names, or chosen by hand); display one of seven predefined compositions (natural colour, colour infrared, agriculture, healthy vegetation, short-wave infrared, urban, geology) or your own bands; calculate the NDVI, NDWI, NDBI and NBR indices in the background.<br><br><b>SV Classification</b>: create the classes, draw the training areas (polygons or circles) that are added to the class with a right click, check the spectral signatures, test the result on a small area with the preview, then classify the whole image with maximum likelihood, minimum distance, spectral angle mapping, random forest, support vector machine or neural network.<br><br><b>Download</b>: search and download Sentinel-2 (Copernicus Data Space and Earth Search on AWS), Landsat, Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2, MODIS, ASTER and Copernicus DEM (Microsoft Planetary Computer, NASA Earthdata), and add your own STAC catalogues.<br><br><b>Settings</b>: English or French, check of the links of the sources, accounts, credits.<br><br><b>Created by ANABA MANGA</b>: the single four-tab panel, the colour compositions and indices, the satellite detection, the class and ROI workflow (polygon and circle, right click to add), the preview, the locking of the options during a processing, the Earth Search source and the sources of your own, the check of the links, the English and French interface and the new design. The processing library Remotior Sensus is bundled, so the processing works offline. Developed and maintained by ANABA MANGA (<a href="https://anbmng.com">anbmng.com</a>), copyright 2026, distributed under the GNU General Public License version 3 or later. The credits are in the Settings tab of the panel.
